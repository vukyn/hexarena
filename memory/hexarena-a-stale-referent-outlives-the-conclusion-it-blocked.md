Three levers on `reckless` were declared dead, and the reason on record for the
third was a **cast-wide** objection: every amount that made the dragon duel a real
matchup also saturated Squirtle at 100%, turning the trait into the
100%-against-the-cast trait `blood_thirst` had been refused for being.

Those figures were measured on a Squirtle whose `withdraw` restored nothing — a
self-aimed skill's `restores` clause paid out until the payout was given a second
caller. `squirtle_test.go` carries the sentence **"Re-take it before quoting the
amount."** Nobody did, and the amount was quoted, twice, in README.md and TODO.md,
as the reason a lever could not be pulled.

Re-taken: Squirtle against the dragon build **93.0% → 28.4%**, `blood_thirst`'s
cast-wide pair **100%/100% → 100%/88.0%**. The objection was gone.

**How to apply.**

- A referent has a **date**, and a balance figure quoted from another session is a
  measurement of an engine that no longer exists. Re-take every referent on the
  same run as the thing being compared to it — which the sweeps here already did
  for A and B inside the duel, and did **not** do for the cast-wide pair, because
  that pair lived in prose rather than in the harness.
- A `⚠️ re-take this` comment is a **debt**, not a disclaimer. Grep for the ones
  that exist before trusting any number near them; writing one down does not stop
  it being quoted.
- A number that blocks a decision earns a re-take before the decision is made, not
  after — the cost of checking is one run of a harness that already exists.

⚠️ The conclusion survived anyway: the lever is still dead, because the *ledger*
says softening `bare` drives its cost negative — the trait's own tempo swallows the
vulnerability. That is luck rather than method. The stale referent had blocked the
right answer for the wrong reason for a release, and the right reason was one sweep
away the whole time. See [[hexarena-two-field-rebalance]] for the neighbouring lesson about measuring one change at a time.
